## How this is loaded into GoHighLevel

Knowledge Base → Add Source → **FAQ**, one entry per Q&A pair below.

One question per entry is deliberate. GHL documents its retrieval as: "The
retrieval pipeline treats every chunk equally, then re-ranks for best match."
One Q&A per chunk means each retrieved chunk is a complete answer. A single long
document gets chunked automatically, and a boundary falling between a question
and its answer produces a confident wrong reply.

Two rules follow from that, and both are load-bearing when editing this file:

1. **Every answer is self-contained.** Never write "as mentioned above" or lean
   on a neighbouring entry; chunks are retrieved alone.
2. **Question lines bundle real phrasings** ("Where are you located? What is your
   address?"), per GHL guidance to anticipate variations.

The demonstration-practice disclosure is its own entry AND a prompt rule, because
a header paragraph would be one chunk among forty-four and rarely retrieved.
