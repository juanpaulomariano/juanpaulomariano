# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, in priority order. Business owners (dental practices, gyms,
med spas, limo services) who want their phones answered and their leads
booked without hiring; they skim, they judge by feel, and they act on the
one thing they can hear or see working. Agency owners evaluating whether
Juan's systems are real before white-labeling him; they read the machinery
(workflow names, canvases, configs) and punish anything staged.

## Product Purpose

juanpaulomariano.com is the portfolio of Juan Paulo Mariano, a GoHighLevel
automation architect. It exists to win client builds: a visitor arrives
skeptical, sees running systems, and sends an inquiry. Success is the
contact form, reached convinced.

## Positioning

Everything shown is a real running system, not a mockup. Live n8n
workflows, real GHL records, a recorded call answered by a deployed Vapi
agent. Neighboring portfolios show screenshots of dashboards; this one
shows the machine running and lets the visitor inspect the receipts
(actual workflow names, actual canvases, stated redactions). Honesty about
what is a demonstration (Bright Hollow is a spec practice, not a client)
is part of the claim.

## Constraints

- Sections must be comprehensible in one laptop viewport (~900px content
  height); long content goes behind opt-in disclosure.
- No invented metrics or staged ROI numbers anywhere; a demonstration
  practice has no honest ROI, so operational facts carry that slot.
- Redaction contract: masked values match verbatim between transcripts and
  screenshots; audio cannot be redacted so calls speak only publishable
  details.
- No live AI demo endpoints (no US number; credit abuse risk). Replay-only
  demonstrations are a deliberate decision, not a gap.
- Repo is private; site deploys from main.

## Brand commitments

- Fonts: Space Grotesk (display), Manrope (body). One red accent #C0392B
  with a single meaning per context.
- The base site grammar is white grounds, hairline rules, numbered
  sections 01–05. Confirmed 2026-08-16: section 03 (Conversation AI) may
  claim its own visual identity (its own ground, cards, devices) while
  keeping the site's fonts; the section must still read as part of one
  site.
- Motion philosophy: play-once-then-rest; nothing loops uninvited; full
  reduced-motion paths. Section 03 carries one signature orchestrated
  moment rather than ambient motion everywhere.
