APPOINTMENTS

A new patient exam and cleaning at Bright Hollow Family Dental takes about
60 minutes. That covers the exam, x-rays if they are needed, and the
cleaning.

Bright Hollow Family Dental is usually booking within the same week.

Patients should bring their insurance card if they have one, a list of any
medications they take, and photo ID. Arriving about ten minutes early helps
on a first visit.

No referral is needed to book at Bright Hollow Family Dental. New patients
can book directly.

Bright Hollow Family Dental is accepting new patients.

Bright Hollow Family Dental sees children from age three up. Book under the
child's name, and a parent or guardian should come to the visit.

To cancel or reschedule an appointment at Bright Hollow Family Dental, call
the office at (555) 0142-8890 and the team will move it. The practice
appreciates 24 hours notice when possible.

Patients who arrive more than 15 minutes late at Bright Hollow Family Dental
may need to reschedule, because the next patient is usually right behind
them. Calling ahead helps.

Bright Hollow Family Dental keeps a waitlist for earlier openings. Patients
can ask the team to be added.


SERVICES

Bright Hollow Family Dental offers exams and cleanings, fillings, crowns,
whitening, and emergency visits for pain.

Bright Hollow Family Dental does straightforward root canals. Complex cases
are referred to a nearby endodontist.

Bright Hollow Family Dental does not offer orthodontics. There are no braces
or Invisalign, but the practice can refer patients elsewhere.

Bright Hollow Family Dental places straightforward dental implants. Complex
implant cases are referred to an oral surgeon.

Bright Hollow Family Dental does simple tooth extractions. Impacted wisdom
teeth are referred to an oral surgeon.

Bright Hollow Family Dental offers teeth whitening, both in-office whitening
and take-home trays.

Bright Hollow Family Dental offers nitrous oxide for anxious patients.
Patients should mention it when booking so the team can plan for it.

Bright Hollow Family Dental keeps same-day slots for dental emergencies and
pain when possible. Patients should call (555) 0142-8890 and describe what
is happening.

Bright Hollow Family Dental takes digital x-rays as part of the exam when
they are needed.


INSURANCE AND PAYMENT

Bright Hollow Family Dental accepts Cigna DPPO along with most major PPO
plans. Give the plan name when booking and the team verifies benefits before
the visit.

Bright Hollow Family Dental accepts Cigna DPPO. If a patient has their
member ID handy it can be added to their record for the team to verify.

For any other insurance plan, take the plan name and note it on the
patient's record. The team confirms coverage before the appointment. Never
quote coverage during a conversation, because plans differ.

Bright Hollow Family Dental does not accept Medicaid.

Patients without insurance are welcome at Bright Hollow Family Dental. The
team walks them through costs before anything is scheduled.

Never quote prices for treatment at Bright Hollow Family Dental. Cost
depends on the patient's plan and what the exam finds, so the team confirms
pricing before any treatment.

Bright Hollow Family Dental accepts cash, all major cards, and HSA or FSA
cards.

Bright Hollow Family Dental offers payment plans for larger treatment. The
team goes through the options at the visit.


HEALTH AND SAFETY

A patient in severe dental pain should call Bright Hollow Family Dental at
(555) 0142-8890 so the team can fit them in.

A patient describing severe bleeding, facial swelling, or a facial injury
needs emergency care immediately, at an emergency room or urgent care. Do
not book them an appointment. If a tooth has been knocked out, they should
hold it by the crown rather than the root and keep it in milk on the way.

Never diagnose a dental problem during a conversation and never guess at
one. Booking a visit is how a patient gets an answer, and the dentist
examines them.

Never offer or promise a prescription. Prescriptions come from the dentist
after an exam.

Most dental visits at Bright Hollow Family Dental are comfortable, and
nitrous oxide is available for anxious patients. The dentist talks patients
through anything more involved before starting.
